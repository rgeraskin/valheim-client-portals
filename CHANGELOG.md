# Changelog

## 0.1.2
- Renaming both ends of a pair (`RenameBoth`) works on dedicated servers: the far end could keep its old name and the pair broke.
- A pair renamed by another player shows its new name at the far end too, not only at the end next to you.

## 0.1.1
- Moved or destroyed portals no longer strand you. The destination is checked behind the loading screen: a portal rebuilt within 50 m under the same name takes the trip there; with none, you go back to the portal you left from, with a message saying why, and the portal leaves your list.
- A known portal destroyed anywhere while you play leaves your list at once.
- Works with teleport-speed mods (QuickTeleport).
- Easier to find in mod managers: searching for "client portal" now finds it in Gale and on Hexium.

## 0.1.0
- Initial release.
